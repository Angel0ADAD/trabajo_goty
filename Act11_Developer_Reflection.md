I have already built the data pipeline and calibrated the anti-hallucination threshold for our legal assistant, and I have worked closely with my team on GitHub since the first hackathon session. We have evaluated the baseline model on our test dataset, but I have not finalized the Hugging Face cloud deployment yet. I have never seen such a reliable rejection mechanism for preventing false legal citations.

If I had more time and computational power, I would fine-tune a bilingual generative model on local municipal codes, and I would design an automated voice interface for non-technical citizens.

If our team had chosen a purely generative chatbot without strict retrieval constraints in Phase 1, our assistant would have hallucinated nonexistent traffic fines, and the judge would have penalized our project during the evaluation.
